# Screenshots to take in the lab (owner's list)

**Why:** Microsoft allows screenshots of its products in tutorial books and websites, if you
follow its rules ("Use of Microsoft Copyrighted Content",
https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/legal/intellectualproperty/copyright/permissions).
Pictures copied from Microsoft's website are not clearly allowed, so we use your own.

## Microsoft's rules (please follow all of them)

- Take the **whole window**. Do not crop or cut out a part. (Resizing is allowed; I do that.)
- Do not draw on it or change it. (I add the labels **below** the picture, not on it.)
- **No splash or opening screen** (the logo screen while the program starts).
- **No people:** no account photo or name. Office 2021 can show the signed-in person's name
  and photo at the top right; sign out of the Microsoft account first, or make sure no name or
  photo shows.
- **No other people's content:** use a blank file, or only the made-up text from the book.
- Every picture will carry the line **"Used with permission from Microsoft"** (I add it).

## How to take one window (Microsoft's steps)

From Microsoft's "Use Snipping Tool to capture screenshots" page (Windows 10 and 11):
1. Open the program window you want and maximize it.
2. Open **Snipping Tool**. Choose **Window** mode, then select **New**, then select the window.
3. Save it:
   - **Windows 11:** select **Save as**, choose the folder and file name, then **Save**.
   - **Windows 10:** select **Save Snip**, type the file name, choose **PNG**, then **Save**.
