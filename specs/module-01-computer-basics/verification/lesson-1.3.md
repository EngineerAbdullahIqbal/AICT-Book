## Wording choices (owner to review)

- **"Computer case"** for the box. Dell calls it the *chassis*. No fetchable official or
  recognised source used "system unit" or "case" verbatim (LearnFree pages have no readable
  text; Lenovo's glossary returned 403). The lesson does not use "system unit".
- **UPS moved to Lesson 1.9.** The spec requires every Lesson 1.3 part to link to one of the four
  jobs (AC-1.3-b); a UPS gives backup power and fits none. Source ready for 1.9: Schneider
  Electric — "UPS which stands for Uninterruptible Power Supply is a device that provides backup
  power to electrical systems during power outages or fluctuations."
  https://www.se.com/us/en/product-category/8000-uninterruptible-power-supply-ups/
- "Webcam" is used for the lab's camera; Code.org lists "Camera" as an input.
