## Plain-language explanations (not quoted; owner to review)

- "App: a program that helps you do one kind of task." Paint is called an app by Microsoft (#7);
  the general wording is a plain explanation.
- "Hardware is the parts you can touch" is a plain way to say "physical parts" (#1).
