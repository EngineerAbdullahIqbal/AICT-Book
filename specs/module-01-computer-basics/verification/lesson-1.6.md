## UNVERIFIED in the lesson (owner to test in Notepad on Windows 11 laptop and Windows 10 lab PC)

- Holding **Shift** and pressing a letter types a capital letter.
- With **Caps lock** on, letters are typed as capitals; pressing it again turns it off.
- **Enter** starts a new line in Notepad.
- **Spacebar** puts a space between words.

No official page stating these behaviours was found; only community Q&A posts.
