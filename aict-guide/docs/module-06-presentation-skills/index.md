# Module 6: Presentation Skills

In Module 5 you learned to make slides. In this module, you learn to **present**: to stand in
front of people and explain your idea clearly and calmly.

You plan a short talk, make slides that help it, and practise your voice and body language. You
learn simple ways to feel less nervous, and how to answer questions. At the end, you give a
3-minute talk with your mobile-shop slides from Lesson 5.12.

**Why it matters:** a job interview, a shop meeting, a college project or a health-awareness
talk all need someone who can explain an idea well.

**Your language:** this book is in English. The same skills work when you speak in Urdu, so
you can give your talks in either language.

**Before you start:** finish Module 5 (MS PowerPoint).

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of Module 6, you can:

1. Say who your audience is and the one main message your talk must give.
2. Plan a short talk with a beginning, a middle and an end.
3. Make slides that help your talk: few words, large text, one idea per slide.
4. Speak clearly: loud enough, not too fast, with pauses.
5. Use body language: stand straight, look at your listeners, use open hands.
6. Use simple ways to control nervousness.
7. Practise a talk with speaker notes, Rehearse Timings and Presenter View.
8. Listen to a question, answer it briefly, and say "I will find out" when you are not sure.
9. Give a 3-minute presentation with slides and get feedback with a checklist.
