## Goal

By the end of this lesson, you can point to the main parts of a computer and say what each one
does.

## Why It Matters

Every part of a computer has a job. In [Lesson 1.1](./what-is-a-computer.mdx), you learned the
four jobs: input, processing, output and storage. Now you will see which part does which job.
When you know the parts, you can follow your teacher's instructions. You can also say clearly
what is wrong if a part stops working.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    accTitle: Computer parts and their jobs
    accDescr: The keyboard, mouse and webcam send input to the processor. The processor sends output to the monitor, speakers and printer. The processor and the storage drive are joined both ways.
    K[Keyboard] --> P[Processor]
    M[Mouse] --> P
    W[Webcam] --> P
    P --> Mo[Monitor]

    P --> Pr[Printer]
    P <--> D[Storage drive]
```

**Diagram in words:** The keyboard, mouse and webcam give input. The processor does the
processing. The monitor, speakers and printer give output. The storage drive keeps information.

## Teacher Demo

Use one lab computer that the whole class can see. **Do not unplug or move any cable.**

1. Point to the keyboard and the mouse. Say: "These give **input**." *The class sees the keyboard
   and the mouse.*
2. Point to the webcam. Say: "This is a small camera. It also gives **input**."
3. Point to the monitor. Say: "This is the monitor. It shows **output**."
4. Point to the speakers or headphones. Say: "These give **output** as sound."
5. Point to the printer. Say: "The printer gives **output** on paper."
6. Point to the computer case. Say: "This box is the computer case. The main parts are inside
   it. We do not open it."
7. Say: "Inside the case is the **processor**. It is a small chip. It works like the computer's
   brain. It does the **processing**."
8. Say: "Inside the case is also the **storage drive**. It keeps software and information, even
   when the power is off. This is **storage**."
9. Show the diagram above. Point to each part, then say its job.

## Student Task

Work in pairs at one lab computer. You need one sheet of paper and a pen.

1. Make a table on your paper with two columns: **Part** and **Job**.
2. Write these parts in the Part column: keyboard, mouse, webcam, monitor, speakers or
   headphones, printer, computer case.
3. For each part, write its job: input, processing, output or storage.
4. Take turns. One student points to a part. The other student says its name and its job.
5. Show your teacher: point to each part on your computer and say its job.

## Check Questions

1. Point to the monitor. What is its job?
   **Answer:** Output. It shows information on the screen.
2. Which part works like the computer's brain? Where is it?
   **Answer:** The processor (CPU). It is inside the computer case.
3. Is the printer an input part or an output part?
   **Answer:** Output. It puts information on paper.

## Common Mistakes

- **Calling the whole box "the CPU"** → The CPU is a small chip inside the box. The box is the
  computer case.
- **Thinking the monitor is the whole computer** → The monitor only shows output. The processor
  and the storage drive are inside the case.
- **Pulling cables to look at the parts** → Never pull or unplug a cable. Point to the parts
  only.
