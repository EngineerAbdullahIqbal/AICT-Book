## Goal

By the end of this lesson, you can keep the headings of a long list on the screen while you
scroll, and unfreeze them again.

## Why It Matters

A school's list of 50 students is longer than the screen. When you scroll down, the headings go
off the top. Soon you forget which column is the grade and which is the fee. A shop's daily
sales register has a column for every day of the month. When you scroll right, the item names
go off the left. Freezing keeps the headings in view, like the name strip at the top of a
register.

## Key Terms

| Term | Simple meaning |
|------|----------------|
| Scroll | Move the sheet up, down, left or right on the screen, with the mouse wheel or the scroll bars. |
| Freeze | Lock some rows or columns so that they stay on the screen while the rest moves. |
| Pane | A part of the window. Freezing splits the sheet into a fixed part and a moving part. |
| Freeze Top Row | Keeps row 1 on the screen while you scroll down. |
| Freeze First Column | Keeps column A on the screen while you scroll right. |
| Freeze Panes | Keeps the rows above and the columns left of the selected cell. |
| Unfreeze Panes | Unlocks everything, so that the whole sheet scrolls again. |

Freezing only changes what you see on the screen. It does not change, move or delete any data.

A school office keeps a list of 50 students, from roll number 1 to 50. The list fills rows 1 to
51. Here are the first rows, one row in the middle and the last row.

| Row | A | B | C | D | E |
|-----|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Roll No | Student Name | Father's Name | Grade | Fee (Rs) |
| 2 | 1 | Ahmed | Abdul Rauf | 6 | 2000 |
| 3 | 2 | Aiman | Arshad Ali | 7 | 2200 |
| 4 | 3 | Ali | Nadeem Akhtar | 8 | 2400 |
| … | … | … | … | … | … |
| 31 | 30 | Musa | Imran Saeed | 10 | 2800 |
| … | … | … | … | … | … |
| 51 | 50 | Zoya | Tariq Aziz | 10 | 2800 |

**See the problem**

1. Open the starter file.
2. Scroll down with the mouse wheel until you see row 51. *Row 51 shows 50, Zoya, Tariq Aziz,
   10 and 2800.* Row 1 has gone off the top. Is 10 the grade or something else? It is hard to
   tell.
3. Scroll back up until row 1 is at the top again, so that you start from the headings.

**Freeze the top row**

4. On the **View** tab, select **Freeze Panes**. *A list of three choices opens.*
5. Select **Freeze Top Row**. This keeps the headings in row 1 on the screen.
6. Scroll down to row 51 again. *Row 1 stays at the top. You can read Grade above 10 and Fee
   (Rs) above 2800.*

**Unfreeze, then freeze again**

7. On the **View** tab, select **Freeze Panes**. *The first choice now says **Unfreeze Panes**,
   because the sheet is frozen.* Select it.
8. Scroll down. *Row 1 goes off the top again.* Now you know how to undo a freeze.
9. Scroll back to the top. Freeze the top row again, as in steps 4 and 5, so that the headings
   stay in view.
10. Save the workbook with the name **Student List**
    ([Lesson 7.2](entering-editing-saving-data.mdx)), so that your work is kept.

Rehana has a general store. Every evening she writes how many of each item she sold that day.
Row 1 has the days, from 1 Oct in B1 to 31 Oct in AF1. Column A has the 40 items, in rows 2 to
41. Here are the corners of the sheet.

| Row | A | B | C | … | AF |
|-----|---|---|---|---|----|
| 1 | Item | 1 Oct | 2 Oct | … | 31 Oct |
| 2 | Atta (10 kg bag) | 2 | 7 | … | 8 |
| 3 | Basmati rice (kg) | 10 | 3 | … | 13 |
| … | … | … | … | … | … |
| 41 | Ballpoint pen | 12 | 8 | … | 3 |

1. Open the starter file.
2. Scroll right until you see column AF. *The item names in column A have gone off the left.*
   You cannot tell which item each number belongs to.
3. Scroll back to column A. On the **View** tab, select **Freeze Panes** > **Freeze First
   Column**.
4. Scroll right to column AF again. Column A stays on the left. Check: AF2 shows 8, so Rehana
   sold 8 bags of atta on 31 Oct.
5. Scroll down to row 41. Row 1 with the days goes off the top. One frozen column is not enough
   for this sheet.
6. On the **View** tab, select **Freeze Panes** > **Unfreeze Panes**.
7. Scroll back to the top-left corner, so that A1 shows. Select B2. B2 sits below row 1 and to
   the right of column A.
8. On the **View** tab, select **Freeze Panes** > **Freeze Panes**. This keeps the rows above
   B2 and the columns left of B2.
9. Scroll down to row 41 and right to column AF.
10. Save the workbook with the name **Sales Log**, so that your work is kept.
11. Check your work: at cell AF41 you can still see 31 Oct at the top and Ballpoint pen at the
    left. AF41 shows 3, so Rehana sold 3 pens on 31 Oct. B41 shows 12, the pens sold on 1 Oct.

## Check Questions

1. You want row 1 and column A to stay on the screen. Which cell do you select before you
   choose **Freeze Panes** > **Freeze Panes**?

   <details>
   <summary>Show answer</summary>

   **Answer:** B2. Excel keeps the rows above it (row 1) and the columns left of it (column A).

   </details>

2. Point to the button that freezes and unfreezes rows and columns.

   <details>
   <summary>Show answer</summary>

   **Answer:** On the **View** tab: **Freeze Panes**. When the sheet is frozen, its list shows
   **Unfreeze Panes**.

   </details>

3. A sheet has a title in row 1 and the headings in row 2. You want both rows to stay. What do
   you do?

   <details>
   <summary>Show answer</summary>

   **Answer:** Select A3, the cell below the two rows. Then select **View** > **Freeze Panes** >
   **Freeze Panes**. **Freeze Top Row** would keep only the title.

   </details>

## Common Mistakes

- **Selecting the wrong cell before Freeze Panes** → The wrong rows or columns stay. Select
  **Unfreeze Panes**. Then select the cell below the rows and right of the columns you want to
  keep, and freeze again.
- **Using Freeze Top Row when the headings are in row 2** → Only row 1 stays. Select A3 and use
  **Freeze Panes** > **Freeze Panes** instead.
- **Thinking the data has gone when part of the sheet will not scroll** → The sheet is frozen.
  Select **View** > **Freeze Panes** > **Unfreeze Panes**.
