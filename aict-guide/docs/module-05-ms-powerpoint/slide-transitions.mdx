## Goal

By the end of this lesson, you can add a transition between slides, apply it to all slides, and preview it.

## Why It Matters

In a slide show, one slide goes away and the next one comes in. A transition is the movement you
see at that moment. A mobile shop can make its new phones slide in one after another. A school
can show sports day pictures with a soft change between them. A good transition makes your
slide show look smooth and finished. Used with care, it helps people follow you.

## Key Terms

| Term | Simple meaning |
|------|----------------|
| Transition | The movement you see when one slide goes away and the next slide comes in. |
| Transitions tab | The ribbon tab with all the transitions and their settings. |
| Transitions gallery | The row of transition pictures on the **Transitions** tab. Each picture is one transition. |
| Preview | A short play of the effect, so you can see it before the slide show. |
| Effect Options | A button that changes how a transition moves, for example the side it comes from. |
| Apply To All | A button that gives the same transition to every slide in the presentation. |
| Duration | How many seconds the transition takes. A bigger number means a slower transition. |

A transition is like turning a page in a book. You can turn it fast, slowly, or from a
different side. The page itself does not change.

A transition belongs to the slide that comes **in**. If slide 2 has a transition, you see it
when slide 1 changes to slide 2.

## Step by Step

Open your **Sports Day** presentation from [Lesson 5.3](./saving-and-slide-show.mdx). It has
three slides. If you cannot find it, make three slides again (see
[Lesson 5.2](./adding-slides-and-text.mdx)).

**Adding a transition to one slide**

1. In the thumbnail pane, select slide 2. A transition shows how this slide comes in, after
   slide 1.
2. Select the **Transitions** tab. *You see the transitions gallery: a row of small pictures.*
   All the transitions are here.
3. In the gallery, select **Gallery**. *Slide 2 plays the transition once in the slide pane.*
   PowerPoint plays it so you can see how it looks.

   :::tip[If your screen looks different]

   If you cannot see **Gallery** in the row, select any other transition you can see. All the
   next steps work the same way.

   :::

4. Select **Effect Options**. Choose **From Right**. Effect Options change the direction or
   style of the movement.
5. Select **Preview**. *The transition plays again, now from the right.* Use **Preview** any time
   you want to watch it again.

**Changing the speed**

6. On the **Transitions** tab, find the **Timing** group. Look at the **Duration** box. The
   number is in seconds.
7. Type a bigger number in the **Duration** box, for example **2**, and press **Enter**. Then
   select **Preview**. *The transition is slower.* A slow transition feels calm. A fast one
   feels quick and sharp.

**Giving all slides the same transition**

8. Select **Apply To All**. *Nothing seems to change, but now every slide has this transition.*
   One transition for all slides makes the presentation look like one set.
9. Select slide 3 in the thumbnail pane. Select **Preview**. *Slide 3 plays the same
   transition.* This shows that **Apply To All** worked.

**Watching it in the slide show**

10. Select the **Slide Show** tab, then **From Beginning** (see
    [Lesson 5.3](./saving-and-slide-show.mdx)). Press **N** to move to the next slide. *Each new
    slide comes in with your transition.*
11. Press **Esc** to end the show.

**Removing a transition**

12. Select slide 3. On the **Transitions** tab, in the gallery, select **None**. Now slide 3 has
    no transition.
13. To remove the transition from every slide, select **None**, then select **Apply To All**.
14. Choose the transition you like best again and select **Apply To All**. Press **Ctrl+S** to
    save, so that your transitions are kept.

{/* Screenshot to add: The Transitions tab in PowerPoint 2021, with the transitions gallery, Effect Options, Preview, the Duration box and Apply To All circled. */}

Use transitions with care. A calm transition helps people follow you. Too much movement takes
their eyes away from your words.

## Practice

Open your **Wedding Plan** presentation from [Lesson 5.3](./saving-and-slide-show.mdx). If you do
not have it, make these three slides again first.

| Slide | Title | Other text |
|-------|-------|------------|
| 1 | Our Family Wedding | Plan for relatives |
| 2 | Mehndi | Thursday evening; At home |
| 3 | Barat | Saturday at 7 pm; Green Garden Hall |

1. Select slide 2 in the thumbnail pane.
2. Select the **Transitions** tab. Select a transition that you like, so that you see its
   preview.
3. Try two or three other transitions. Keep the one that feels calm and neat.
4. Select **Effect Options** and choose another option. Select **Preview** to see the change.
5. Type **1.5** in the **Duration** box and press **Enter**, so that the change is not too slow.
6. Select **Apply To All**, so that every slide uses the same transition.
7. Start the slide show from the beginning. Press the **Spacebar** to move through the slides.
   Press **Esc** at the end.
8. Press **Ctrl+S** to save your work.
9. Check your work: in the slide show, every slide comes in with the same transition. When you
   select slide 2 or 3 and select **Preview**, you see that same transition.

## Check Questions

1. Point to the button that gives the same transition to every slide.

   <details>
   <summary>Show answer</summary>

   **Answer:** **Apply To All**, on the **Transitions** tab.

   </details>

2. Slide 3 has a transition. When do you see it in the slide show?

   <details>
   <summary>Show answer</summary>

   **Answer:** When slide 2 changes to slide 3. A transition shows how a slide comes in.

   </details>

3. How do you take a transition off a slide?

   <details>
   <summary>Show answer</summary>

   **Answer:** Select the slide. On the **Transitions** tab, in the gallery, select **None**.

   </details>

## Common Mistakes

- **A different transition on every slide** → Choose one calm transition and select **Apply To
  All**, so that the slides look like one set.
- **Selecting the wrong slide before choosing a transition** → Select the slide that should come
  in with the effect, then choose the transition.
- **A transition that is too slow** → Type a smaller number in the **Duration** box.
- **Thinking the transition did not work** → Select **Preview**, or start the slide show. The
  transition only plays when the slide comes in.
