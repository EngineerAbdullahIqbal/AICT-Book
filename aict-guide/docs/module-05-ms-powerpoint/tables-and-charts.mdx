## Goal

By the end of this lesson, you can insert a table and a simple column chart on a slide, and type their data.

## Why It Matters

Some facts are clearer in rows and columns. A mobile shop can show three phones and their
prices in a small table. A tuition centre can show its fees for each grade. Other facts are
clearer as a picture. A chart shows at a glance which month had the most sales, or which house
won the most points on sports day. You made tables in Word in
[Lesson 4.9](../module-04-ms-word/tables.mdx) and charts in Excel in
[Lesson 7.11](../module-07-ms-excel/simple-chart.mdx). Now you put them on slides.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    accTitle: How to add a column chart to a slide
    accDescr: Select the Insert tab, then Chart. Choose Column, then Clustered Column. A small worksheet opens. Type your data in it, then close the worksheet.
    A[Insert tab] --> B[Chart]
    B --> C[Column]

    E --> F[Close the worksheet]
```

**Diagram in words:** Select the Insert tab, then Chart. Choose Column, then Clustered Column.
Type your data in the small worksheet that opens, then close the worksheet.

## Key Terms

| Term | Simple meaning |
|------|----------------|
| Table | Boxes in rows and columns that hold words or numbers, like a timetable. |
| Cell | One box in a table or worksheet, where a row and a column meet. |
| Chart | A picture made from numbers. It helps you compare them quickly. |
| Column chart | A chart with standing bars. A taller column means a bigger number. |
| Clustered Column | The plain, flat kind of column chart. |
| Worksheet | A small Excel sheet that holds the numbers for your chart. |
| Sample data | Made-up names and numbers that PowerPoint puts in the worksheet. You type your own data over them. |

A chart in PowerPoint keeps its numbers in a small Excel worksheet. You learned about worksheets
and cells in [Lesson 7.1](../module-07-ms-excel/starting-excel.mdx).

## Step by Step

Open PowerPoint and start a blank presentation (see [Lesson 5.1](./starting-powerpoint.mdx)).
You will make slides for Bright Mobile Shop. On slide 1, type the title **Bright Mobile Shop**
and the line **Our new phones**.

**A table of prices**

1. Add a new slide with the **Title and Content** layout (see
   [Lesson 5.2](./adding-slides-and-text.mdx)). Type the title **Prices**.
2. Select the **Insert** tab, then select **Table**. *You see a grid of small squares.*
3. Move the mouse over the grid until 2 columns and 4 rows are marked. Then click. *An empty
   table with 2 columns and 4 rows appears on the slide.* You need one row for the headings and
   three rows for the phones.

   :::tip[If your screen looks different]

   If the grid is hard to use, select **Insert Table** instead. Type **2** in
   **Number of columns** and **4** in **Number of rows**. Then press **Enter**.

   :::

4. Click the first cell of the table and type **Phone**. Click the next cell to the right and
   type **Price (Rs)**. These headings tell people what each column means.
5. Click each cell in turn and type the rest of the table:

   | Phone | Price (Rs) |
   |-------|------------|
   | Phone A | 24,500 |
   | Phone B | 32,000 |
   | Phone C | 41,000 |

6. Click outside the table when you finish. *You see the table with your words and prices.*

**A column chart of sales**

The shop sold some phones each month. A chart shows which month was best.

7. Add a new slide with the **Title and Content** layout. Type the title **Phones Sold**.
8. Select the **Insert** tab, then select **Chart**. *The **Insert Chart** box opens.*
9. Select **Column**. Then double-click **Clustered Column**. *A column chart appears on the
   slide. A small worksheet opens with sample data.* The chart shows this sample data until you
   type your own.
10. In the worksheet, type this data over the sample data, starting in cell A1. Press **Enter**
    after each entry. *The chart changes each time you press **Enter**.*

    | | A | B |
    |---|---|---|
    | **1** | Month | Phones sold |
    | **2** | January | 40 |
    | **3** | February | 55 |
    | **4** | March | 48 |
    | **5** | April | 62 |

11. The worksheet may also have sample numbers in columns C and D. You do not need them. Select
    the chart. *In the worksheet, the cells used by the chart are marked, with sizing handles.*
    Drag the handle at the lower-right corner to the left, until only columns A and B are
    marked. *The chart now shows only your data.*

    :::tip[If your screen looks different]

    If the chart still shows extra columns, right-click the chart and choose **Select Data**.
    In the list, deselect each item you do not need, then select **OK**.

    :::

12. Close the worksheet window with its **Close** button (**X**). *You see the slide with your
    chart.* You can open the worksheet again later if a number changes.

{/* Screenshot to add: PowerPoint 2021 with a column chart on a slide and the small chart worksheet open above it, showing Month and Phones sold in columns A and B. */}

**Giving the chart a title**

13. Select the chart's title box. Select it again, then drag over the words in it to select
    them. Type **Phones Sold, January to April**. A title tells people what the chart shows.

    If you see no title box, select the **+** sign at the top-right of the chart. Then select
    the **Chart Title** check box.

14. Look at the chart. April has the tallest column, 62 phones. January has the shortest, 40
    phones. The chart shows this in one look.
15. To change a number later, select the chart. On the **Chart Design** tab, select **Edit
    Data**. The worksheet opens again.
16. Save the presentation as **Phone Shop** (see [Lesson 5.3](./saving-and-slide-show.mdx)),
    so that your work is kept.

Keep tables and charts small. A few rows, short labels and clear numbers can be read from
the back of a room.

## Practice

Green Valley School wants slides for its sports day results. Make a table of winners and a
chart of house points.

| Race | Winner |
|------|--------|
| 100 metre race | Ayesha, Red House |
| Sack race | Bilal, Blue House |
| Tug of war | Green House |

| House | Points |
|-------|--------|
| Red | 45 |
| Blue | 60 |
| Green | 38 |
| Yellow | 52 |

1. Open PowerPoint and select **Blank Presentation**. Type the title **Sports Day Results**.
2. Add a **Title and Content** slide with the title **Winners**.
3. Insert a table with 2 columns and 4 rows (**Insert**, **Table**). Type the first table above
   into it, with the headings **Race** and **Winner**.
4. Add another **Title and Content** slide with the title **House Points**.
5. Insert a chart (**Insert**, **Chart**). Select **Column**, then double-click **Clustered
   Column**.
6. In the worksheet, type the second table above, starting in cell A1. Use **House** and
   **Points** as the headings.
7. If the worksheet has extra sample columns, make the marked cells cover only columns A and B.
8. Close the worksheet. Give the chart the title **House Points**.
9. Save the presentation as **Sports Results**.
10. Check your work: slide 2 has a table with 4 rows: the headings and three races. Slide 3 has
    a column chart with 4 columns. Blue (60 points) has the tallest column and Green (38
    points) has the shortest. The chart title says **House Points**.

## Check Questions

1. Point to the tallest column in your **Phones Sold** chart. Which month is it?

   <details>
   <summary>Show answer</summary>

   **Answer:** April, with 62 phones sold.

   </details>

2. You need a table with 3 columns and 5 rows. How do you insert it?

   <details>
   <summary>Show answer</summary>

   **Answer:** On the **Insert** tab, select **Table**. Move the mouse over the grid until 3
   columns and 5 rows are marked, then click. Or select **Insert Table** and type 3 and 5.

   </details>

3. Your chart has a wrong number. How do you open its worksheet again?

   <details>
   <summary>Show answer</summary>

   **Answer:** Select the chart. On the **Chart Design** tab, select **Edit Data**.

   </details>

## Common Mistakes

- **Leaving the sample data in the worksheet** → Type your own data over the sample data. Remove
  the extra sample columns from the chart.
- **Pressing Tab in the last cell of the table** → This adds a new row. Press **Ctrl+Z** to
  undo it.
- **The chart title still shows the old words** → Select the title box, select it again, select
  the words, and type your own title.
- **Looking for Edit Data on the Design tab** → First select the chart. Then use the **Chart
  Design** tab, which appears only when a chart is selected.
